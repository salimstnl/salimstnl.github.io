Cognitive Mapping & Learning Species

Human cognition is not a monolithic structure. Through my work building Vitademy, I realised that the friction students feel when learning complex mathematics rarely comes from a lack of intelligence; it comes from a mismatch in cognitive mapping.

By evaluating 5 core dimensions, we can isolate exactly how a person visualises abstraction and constructs logic. This is the foundation of identifying your 'Learning Species'.

When you understand your own architectural framework, learning stops being a battle of brute-force memorisation and becomes a strategic game of pattern recognition.

The Art of Learning Under Constraints

Balancing a startup, academic research, and personal life is a classic optimisation problem. We often mistake forcing 240 hours of workload into a 24-hour window as ambition, but mathematically, it is just greed.

True productivity requires strategic patience. It requires acknowledging the constraints of the system and finding the lowest-energy path through it. Growth happens during the silent, constrained struggles, not the chaotic sprints.

Art vs. Science: What Exactly is Mathematics?

Society views mathematics as a rigid science of absolute truths. Yet, those who study it at the highest levels understand it is profoundly artistic.

The numbers and symbols are merely the medium, much like paint to a canvas. The true essence of mathematics is the creative leap required to connect two seemingly unrelated structures, turning chaos into elegant symmetry.
